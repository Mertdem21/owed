# Northwind Electronics - Price Adjustment and Return Policy

Effective September 1, 2026. Demo policy of a fictional retailer.

## Price Adjustment

If Northwind Electronics lowers the price of an item you bought from us within 14 days of your purchase date, we will refund the difference to your original payment method.

The item must be identical (same SKU) and sold and shipped by Northwind Electronics.

Price adjustments do not apply to items sold by Marketplace sellers, clearance items, or open-box items.

Each item can receive one price adjustment. Request a price adjustment through the Northwind claims portal with your order number.

## Returns

Most items can be returned within 15 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.

Items must include all original accessories and packaging.

## Billing Questions

If you were charged incorrectly, contact Northwind support within 60 days of the charge.
