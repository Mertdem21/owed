# Brightline Home - Billing and Refund Policy

Effective July 1, 2026. Demo policy of a fictional retailer.

## Billing Errors

If you were charged more than once for the same order, Brightline Home will refund every duplicate charge in full.

Report a duplicate charge within 60 days of the charge date. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Returns

Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Custom and personalized items cannot be returned.
